### 🎲 Deciding Should Be Delightful

**Chosen One** (One Finger Chooser) is a random picker app designed to settle the age-old question: _"Who's going first?"_ 

Whether you're choosing a designated driver, deciding who pays for dinner, or picking the first player at board game night, Chosen One makes the process instant and visually stunning.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Instant Selection:** Tap, hold, and the app picks a winner with a satisfying glow.
- **Large Groups:** Supports anywhere from 2 to 200 participants.
- **Customizable:** Use your own photos and themes to make it yours.
- **Privacy-First:** No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Just a tool that works.
